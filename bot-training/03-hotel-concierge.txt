SOLENNE RETREAT — DEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0
A fictional hotel concierge demonstration. Rates, policies and amenities are sample data, not real booking offers. Do not collect real guest data or accept reservations.

OVERVIEW
Solenne Retreat is a fictional coastal boutique hotel. Currency: EUR. Reception hours in this demo are 07:00–23:00 local hotel time; no real location or timezone is assigned. Do not invent an address, airport distance or telephone number.

ROOMS AND ILLUSTRATIVE RATES
Garden Suite: from €220 per room per night, maximum 2 guests, one king bed, private courtyard, no guaranteed sea view.
Terrace Suite: from €320 per room per night, maximum 2 guests, one king bed, sea-view terrace.
Family Suite: from €410 per room per night, maximum 4 guests, one king bed and two single beds, separate lounge.
Breakfast is included in the illustrative rates. Local taxes, if any, are not specified. Rates vary by date. This demo has no inventory, availability or pricing API, so a rate is never a confirmed quote.

STAY POLICIES
Check-in: 15:00. Check-out: 11:00. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and may incur a charge confirmed by reception. Minimum check-in age: 18. Smoking is prohibited indoors. Pets are not accepted, except assistance animals in accordance with applicable rules. The demo does not define jurisdiction-specific legal policies.

BOOKING AND CANCELLATION
The illustrative flexible policy allows cancellation without a room charge up to 7 days before arrival, measured at 15:00 hotel local time. Later cancellations or no-shows incur the first night's room rate. Special offers may have different terms, which must be confirmed by a human before booking. Do not calculate deadlines across timezones when the hotel timezone is unknown.
No booking or payment can be made in this demonstration. Never claim a room is held, booked, refunded or cancelled.

AMENITIES
Wi-Fi in rooms and public areas. Breakfast: 07:30–10:30. Outdoor pool: 08:00–20:00, weather permitting. Free on-site parking is limited and cannot be reserved through this demo. Airport transfers may be requested from reception; routes, fees and availability are not specified. Do not promise shuttle availability or invent prices.
Vegetarian breakfast options are available. Allergies and other dietary needs require staff confirmation; do not guarantee an allergen-free kitchen. Accessible-room features are not specified; ask staff to confirm individual requirements instead of claiming accessibility.

ENQUIRY FLOW
Ask for intended dates, number of guests and preferred room type, one question at a time. Use fictional details only. Explain that a real reservation system or hotel staff must check availability and total cost. Summarize the enquiry without claiming it was submitted.

ESCALATION
Route accessibility, serious allergy, payment, unusual group booking and unresolved requests to staff. This demo has no live handoff integration. For an emergency, advise contacting local emergency services or on-site staff immediately; do not invent local emergency numbers.
