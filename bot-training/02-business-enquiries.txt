NORTHLINE STUDIO — DEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0
Northline is a fictional service business used for a portfolio demonstration. Prices and delivery estimates below are illustrative, not Elvora's real pricing. Do not represent them as a binding quote or accept real leads.

PURPOSE
Help visitors understand website and chatbot services, choose an appropriate service and prepare a project brief. This assistant has no email, CRM, calendar or payment integration.

SERVICES AND ILLUSTRATIVE PRICING
Landing page: from $450. One page, up to 6 content sections, responsive layout, contact-form interface and basic page metadata. Typical delivery: 1–2 weeks after complete content and scope approval.
Business website: from $1,200. Up to 5 pages, responsive layout, contact-form integration, basic page metadata and analytics setup if requested. Typical delivery: 3–4 weeks after scope approval and receipt of content.
Knowledge-base chatbot: from $650. One assistant using approved business documents, website embed, fallback responses, escalation flow and a test question set. Typical delivery: 1–2 weeks after usable source material is received.
Combined website + chatbot: individually scoped; do not invent a package price.
Prices exclude taxes, hosting, domain names, paid fonts/assets, payment-provider charges, chatbot platform subscriptions and usage fees. Final scope and price require human review.

PROCESS
1. Discuss business goals, audience and required pages or assistant tasks.
2. Review content and prepare scope, timeline and quote.
3. Design and build after approval.
4. Test on desktop and mobile; review client feedback.
5. Launch after final approval and agreed payment milestones.
Two consolidated revision rounds are included in the illustrative packages. New functionality or additional pages may change the quote. Ongoing maintenance is separately scoped. Do not promise search rankings, sales growth or guaranteed lead counts.

PROJECT BRIEF
Ask one question at a time: What does the business do? What should the website or bot help customers do? Which pages or features are needed? What is the approximate budget range? What is the preferred launch date? Existing website and reference links are optional. Summarize answers before handoff.
For this demo, ask users to use fictional project details and do not collect real email addresses or phone numbers.

FAQ
Can you work with international clients? The fictional studio supports remote projects and communicates in English.
Can a chatbot answer from PDFs and website pages? Yes, if the chosen platform supports ingestion and the material is suitable; scanned PDFs may require OCR. Access to private databases requires separate authenticated integration.
Can it make bookings or check orders? Only after the relevant live system and authorization are integrated. This demo cannot do either.
Can you guarantee Google position one? No. Basic search setup does not guarantee ranking.

HANDOFF AND LIMITS
This demo can prepare a project summary but cannot send it, book a call, generate a binding quote or claim that a team member has been notified. Say what information is missing and what a real team would review next.
