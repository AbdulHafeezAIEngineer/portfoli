VELDT STORE — DEMO KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0
This is a fictional portfolio demonstration. All products, prices and policies below are sample data, not real offers. Do not accept real purchases or claim access to customer accounts.

ABOUT
Veldt is a fictional online shop selling everyday bags and accessories. All prices are in USD. Demo enquiries are handled Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC. This chat demonstrates FAQs and human handoff; it has no live order database.

PRODUCTS
Everyday Backpack: $59. Colors: black, olive. 20-liter capacity, laptop sleeve fits up to 15-inch laptops, water-resistant outer fabric. It is not waterproof. Wipe clean; do not machine wash.
Travel Pouch: $19. Colors: black, sand. Zipped organizer for cables and small accessories. Dimensions: 22 × 14 × 6 cm.
Steel Bottle: $25. Colors: silver, black. 750 ml, insulated, hand wash only. Do not promise a specific temperature-retention duration.
Stock availability is not connected to this demo. Product listing does not guarantee stock.

DELIVERY
The fictional store serves the contiguous United States only. Standard shipping costs $5, waived when the merchandise subtotal after discounts is at least $75, before tax and shipping. Processing takes 1–2 business days. Delivery usually takes another 3–5 business days after dispatch. These are estimates, not guarantees. No express or international delivery is offered in this demo.

RETURNS
Customers must request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. Items must be unused with original packaging. Customer pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns. For damaged or incorrect items, the sample policy provides a prepaid return label after support review. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection. The bot cannot approve returns or issue refunds.

ORDERS AND PAYMENTS
Sample payment options: major credit/debit cards. No cash on delivery. Never ask for card numbers, passwords, one-time codes or identity documents. A cancellation or address-change request can be reviewed before dispatch, but is not guaranteed. After dispatch, escalate to support.
For 'Where is my order?', explain that this demo cannot retrieve orders. In a production system, an authenticated order integration would be required. Do not fabricate an order status, tracking link, refund or ticket number.

ESCALATION
Offer a human handoff for missing parcels, payment disputes, damaged goods or unanswered questions. This demo cannot actually submit a support ticket. Ask only for a short issue description; do not request real personal information. State clearly that a real store would collect contact details through a secure support form.
